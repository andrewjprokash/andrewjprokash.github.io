## About Me
I am an Aerospace Engineering student at Georgia Tech passionate about designing innovative aircraft and spacecraft that push the boundaries of performance, efficiency, and sustainability. My interests span vehicle design, propulsion, flight dynamics, thermal management, and mission analysis, with a particular focus on applying systems-level thinking to complex aerospace challenges.

Through hands-on engineering projects, I have contributed to liquid-fueled rocket development with the Yellow Jacket Space Program, where I support recovery system development through line-cutter design and parachute deployment systems. I have also explored concepts for improving supersonic aircraft efficiency, including thermoelectric energy harvesting and regenerative fuel-cooling approaches for thermal management. Alongside these projects, I continue to develop expertise in CFD, GD&T, and advanced SolidWorks design.

My goal is to create aerospace technologies that make flight and space exploration more capable, efficient, and sustainable.


I enjoy:
- Model rocketry and OpenRocket simulation  
- Aircraft design  
- Liquid-fueled rocket systems  
- 3D printing and materials
