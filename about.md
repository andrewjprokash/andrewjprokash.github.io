## About Me
I’m an Aerospace Engineering student at Georgia Tech specializing in flight dynamics, rocketry, and vehicle recovery systems. 
I currently serve on the YJSP Elytra Flight Dynamics team, where I research ejection mechanisms for our LOX/RP‑1 spaceshot vehicle, Elytra, 
and calculate black‑powder charge sizing for recovery deployment. My engineering interests include rocket stability, CAD modeling, 
and hands-on prototyping.


I enjoy:
- Model rocketry and OpenRocket simulation  
- Glider and aircraft design  
- Liquid-fueled rocket systems  
- 3D printing and materials (PLA)

This portfolio documents my engineering work, design process, and technical growth.
